Official California Overtime Rules Implemented:

  • Daily Overtime: 1.5x regular rate for hours worked over 8.0 up to 12.0 in a single workday.
  • Daily Double Time: 2.0x regular rate for hours worked over 12.0 in a single workday.
  • Weekly Overtime: 1.5x regular rate for hours worked over 40.0 regular hours in a workweek.
  • 7th Consecutive Day Rule: 1.5x regular rate for the first 8 hours worked on the 7th consecutive day of a workweek, and 2.0x regular rate for any hours exceeding 8 on that day.

California Overtime Law & Compliance Guide

Understanding wage compliance rules enforced by the California Department of Industrial Relations (DIR) can be complex. Unlike federal standards governed under the FLSA, California labor law applies strict thresholds that calculate extra compensation on both a daily and weekly basis.

How Does the CA Overtime Calculator Work?

This automated interface processes multi-tier tracking sequences according to active state industrial codes. To use the tool, simply enter your base regular wage structure followed by the exact block of hours registered across your active 7-day shifts. The script parses standard regular, time-and-a-half (1.5x), and double-time (2.0x) distributions simultaneously.

What is the California Daily Overtime Rule?

In the state of California, an employer must compensate non-exempt personnel at a rate of 1.5 times their regular base salary for any hours logged beyond 8.0 hours in a single designated workday. Furthermore, weekly thresholds stipulate that any labor tracking past 40.0 total regular rate hours across a single workweek automatically transitions into standard overtime tracking.

When Does Daily Double Time Apply in California?

Double time (2x regular rate) is triggered when a shift surpasses extreme daily lengths. Non-exempt hourly workers are legally entitled to double their baseline pay structure for any hours worked past 12.0 hours in a single calendar workday.

Understanding the 7th Consecutive Day Overtime Rule

The 7th consecutive day overtime rule is a frequent source of payroll errors. If an employee is required to work all seven consecutive days inside a defined employer workweek, specific structural protections change:

  • The first 8.0 hours logged on that 7th consecutive working day must be compensated at the 1.5x overtime premium rate.
  • Any hours worked past 8.0 on that 7th consecutive working day escalate immediately to the 2.0x double-time rate.

What is the Minimum Wage in California?

As of 2026, the baseline California state minimum wage is strictly regulated at $16.90 per hour for all non-exempt workers across all operational corporate structures. Local city or county ordinances may enforce higher localized baselines, which take legal precedence over the state floor.
